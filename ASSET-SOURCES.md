Removed in this build: all Mixamo-derived FBX files (character, animations, enemy), plus unreferenced files of unknown origin (tree.glb, sword.glb).

## Potion and scabbard (replacement, 2026-11-21)
- Potion: now PotionCC0.glb (Quaternius Ultimate RPG Items Pack, CC0 notice in the original zip, see ATTACH-LICENSE-SOURCE.txt). The old template Potion.glb (unknown origin) was removed.
- Scabbard: ScabbardOwn.glb is an original procedural model made for this project (tapered flat cylinder, brass collar and bands), no third party source. The old template Scabbard.glb (unknown origin) was removed. The scabbard slot is kept.
- Placement checked in close-up renders (frames/attach-*.png): scabbard hangs vertically at the hip (Hips rotation [0,90,0]), potion flask stands upright on the belt (Spine position [-0.17,0.17,-0.03], rotation [0,0,-90]).

## Guns (verified 2026-11-21)
- P90CC0.glb and RifleCC0.glb come from Quaternius "Low Poly Animated Guns" (FPS Pack), https://opengameart.org/content/low-poly-animated-guns (page: author quaternius, posted 2017-09-17, license CC0). The archive "FPS Pack_0.zip" downloaded today has SHA-256 1af9e37e9852dea6bb5e1db8b93a057e067e740083a5ba48e6bb13fc8d19ccaf, equal to the recorded value. The vertex bounding boxes of OBJ/P90.obj and OBJ/Rifle.obj in that archive equal those of the two GLBs (converted with three.js, no edits). Released in 2017, before the 2026 QAL license.

## Font, CPK kit (verified 2026-11-21)
- NanumGothic-Regular.ttf: SHA-256 76f45ef4a6bcff344c837c95a7dcc26e017e38b5846d5ae0cdcb5b86be2e2d31, identical to google/fonts ofl/nanumgothic (METADATA license OFL). OFL.txt (NHN Corporation) ships beside it.
- Cyberpunk Game Kit (public/models/environment/cpk, enemy and environment models): official page https://quaternius.com/packs/cyberpunkgamekit.html, July 2022 release, CC0 (also LICENSE-CPK.txt inside the archive). Released before the 2026 QAL license. Byte download from the official Drive folder (https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1GyKLypoBxrpLN6GERF9U610Wiub48v9y, linked from the pack page) was blocked by Drive quota, so no hash comparison. Instead, all 66 shipped .gltf files were matched by file name and size (to the KB shown by Drive) against the entries in that official folder (Enemies, Pickups and Objects, Platforms; all dated Jul 11-12, 2022); no shipped file lacks a listing entry and none differs in size. License.txt in the folder is 368 bytes; the shipped LICENSE-CPK.txt is 356 bytes (not byte-identical, text not compared).

## UAL (verified 2026-11-21)
- public/animations/cc0/ual.glb is byte-identical (SHA-256 69591853d817488edaa8fd9bf8fc1d821eaeaf789f8627b3cd23b41c4ed67997) to Unreal-Godot/UAL1_Standard.glb inside "Universal Animation Library[Standard].zip", downloaded from the official itch.io page https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library (free "name your own price" file, no payment or account; file listed there as Jun 16, 2026, 15 MB). The zip's License.txt says CC0 1.0, as does the pack page https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html. Both dates are before the QAL license (updated 2026-08-28, not retroactive).
- The earlier ual.glb in this repo (different rig names, 46 clips including Punch_Enter, Roll_RM, Sword_Attack_RM that are not in the official Standard file) could not be tied to an official file and was replaced by the official file. The retargeter now uses the official Unreal-style bone names (RetargetCC0.js UE_NAME map). Retargeted clips differ from the old ones by about 1 degree on average (up to 20 degrees on some bones; death clip up to 70 degrees on one leg bone), because the pack was revised.
